# Qalam: Word by Word — deep audit, 23 Nov 2026

How it was done: eight read-only auditors (one view each), every finding re-checked against the code by a second
skeptical reviewer, plus a live play of rooms 1–3 at phone (390×844, English and Urdu) and short-wide (844×390) sizes.
125 findings were raised; refuted ones are dropped below. 68 unit tests pass. No game code was changed.

## What works
- First minute is clear: goal line, language pick, a what-to-do line on every step, gentle wrong taps, Show me.
- No console errors, no stuck screens, nothing overflows in the first three rooms at the sizes played.
- Content pipeline, save schema, spaced review, look-alike wrong answers and warm-ups are in place.

## The 12 problems that matter most

### Reliability (progress can be lost)
1. **A finished room is not saved until the last ▶ is tapped.** Closing the game, or tapping Map, on the "Room lamp
   lit" screen throws the win away and the whole Last Check must be redone. `ui/room.js:231-263`, `main.js:111`.
2. **A failed cloud load starts a returning player from zero**, with saving off for the session and no retry.
   Failed saves are also ignored silently. No time limit on any SDK or network call, so a hang is a frozen lamp.
3. **Relight, warm-up and Last Check answers are saved only at the end.** Any interruption loses them.
4. **No safety net.** A thrown error in a step leaves a dead screen. A long-press tip can swallow the next tap.

### Learning (the player does not really learn the word)
5. **Too much, too early.** Room 1 teaches 13 words in 66 taps, room 2 teaches 17. The Last Check needs 26 then 34
   right answers; 14 of the 17 room-2 words were only seen once and never practised before being tested.
6. **77 calligraphy words (Allah, ar-Rahman, Rabb …) are tested by matching the same Arabic to itself.** Their
   meaning is never checked.
7. **Help gives the answer away and still counts as known.** The tutorial hand points at the right answer on the
   first question of every step type and it is graded as unaided. First Show me reveals the full answer (studio rule
   is second request). Tapping an ayah tile again wipes a miss.
8. **Review is weak.** A missed Relight word costs nothing; replaying a finished room levels every word up; Relight
   always serves 12 and says "All lamps are lit" with more due; mix-ups are thrown away on save; no free practice.
9. **A wrong tap teaches nothing.** It says "Not quite"; it never shows what the tapped picture means.
10. **No way to say the word.** No audio (blocked on rights) and no transliteration toggle, though the brief promises one.

### Content (wrong or unfair answers)
11. **Mis-tagged ayah words.** 20:82 صَالِحًا "good deed" shows the Prophet Salih card; ذٰلِكَ / أُولٰئِكَ / هٰذِهِ are
    graded as "this" from Wing 3 on (incl. 2:2, 2:5); الَّذِينَ graded as singular; 26:115 إِنْ "not" graded as "if";
    7:112 عَلِيمٍ (a magician) uses the Name-of-Allah medallion; سَوْفَ glossed "very soon" in Urdu.
12. **Two answers that look the same, one marked wrong.** Same painting on two words, same Urdu caption on two chips,
    same Arabic word twice in an ayah, and with captions off 39 questions show two identical pictures.

## Beginner-eye notes from live play
- The bottom half of a phone screen is empty; answers sit at the top, out of thumb reach.
- Meaning captions are 9.6–11.8 px, and fit-to-screen can shrink them 30% more. Urdu Nastaliq is squeezed and clipped.
- Snap and Point instructions are hard ("▲ = a he-word, ● = a she-word").
- Emoji (🖐 🎁 👆) sit beside painted art.
- "3 lamps to relight" shows right after the first room, twice on the map.
- At 844×390 the intro Start button is cut off. Settings cannot scroll on small or landscape screens.
- Two Start presses before the first question. Urdu players must pick Urdu again every visit.
- The win screen is a static card; gold text on cream is 2.4–2.8:1 contrast.
- The map is about 8,700 px of look-alike doors with no overview.

## Suggested order of work
- **Phase A — never lose progress (small):** save on win, retry and time limits on load/save, save inside Relight and
  Last Check, error screen with "try again", long-press fix, map-door lock while loading.
- **Phase B — fair grading (small):** hand and Show me count as hinted, two-step hint, tile re-tap keeps the miss,
  Relight lapse and hint grading, no level-up on replay, no identical options, 4 options and shuffled positions.
- **Phase C — content fixes (small, needs your eye on glosses):** the mis-tags above plus a build check that ties
  each ayah word's picture to its gloss.
- **Phase D — gentler start (medium):** split rooms 1–2 to 6–8 new words, practise every word before the check,
  easier check in Wing 1, meaning shown for calligraphy words, show the right pairing after a miss, backlog wiring,
  mix-ups saved (save v4), free practice.
- **Phase E — look and feel (medium):** bigger captions and Arabic, thumb-zone layout, Urdu line-height, contrast,
  win celebration, pressed states, painted icons in place of emoji, settings scroll, short-wide intro, map overview.
- **Phase F — speed and cert (small):** Urdu font only for Urdu, preload next pictures and Qur'an font, no text
  select / double-tap zoom, `dvh` fallback, refresh CERT_EVIDENCE.
- **Needs you:** transliteration yes/no, English translation choice (1930s wording now), gloss decisions marked
  "owner", painted icon set, remembered language (needs a save field), scholar review, recitation audio.

### Closing the game on the "Room lamp lit" screen loses the lamp and the whole Last Check
*high · confirmed · effort S*
