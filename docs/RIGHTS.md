## Where the credits live

- Built into `pack.json` → `credits` by `scripts/lib/pack.mjs`, shown in Settings in this order: Qur'an text, translations, word data, pictures, fonts.
- Decision 2026-11-06 (Khadin): **keep Yusuf Ali** for English. The credit line no longer says "Both public domain" (that claim could not be backed for the US); it now says "Shown unchanged". Still open before release: email Tanzil about the edition (draft no. 1, not sent) and settle the US status.
- Earlier choice (2026-11-05 morning): Pickthall (EN) and Jalandhry (UR); their files stay in `data/source/translations/`, unused.
- To swap a translation (for example, to Pickthall, or to Mufti Taqi Usmani's with his publisher's written permission), change the two files in `data/source/translations/`, `TRANSLATIONS` in `scripts/lib/pack.mjs`, and `credits.translation`.
